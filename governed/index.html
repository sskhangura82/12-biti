Built Governed ✓

Software from Governed Studio ships with its evidence.

Every app on this page was made by one family’s software studio: AI workers do the building under strict, written limits; a human makes every decision that matters; independent checks happen before anything is released; and records are kept so nothing is lost or forgotten. The Pledge

Applies to every app below.

  • No ads, ever.
  • No tracking or analytics.
  • No accounts required.
  • No collection or sale of your data.
  • No dark patterns — nothing designed to trick, pressure, or hook you.
  • Nothing is published or changed without a human’s explicit act.

Courtyard Games

The one link our family plays on.

What it is: classic courtyard games — 12 Biti, Ludo, Saanp Seedhi, X·O — rebuilt as polished web games, all living on a single hub page.

How it’s kept honest: every release is pinned by cryptographic hash and verified at both the repository and the live site before it counts as shipped.

Your data: on this site the games play on one screen or against the computer — nothing leaves your device, and we run no servers. Far-away rooms exist only on the Claude-hosted copy of these games; there, the 4-letter room code and the game state pass through Claude’s shared storage, which is not ours. We store nothing about you either way.

Known limits, stated plainly: there is no far-away play on this site — only on the Claude-hosted copy, where both players must be online at the same time. 12 Biti and X·O have rooms; Ludo and Saanp Seedhi are one-screen only.

Source: github.com/sskhangura82/12-biti.

ClinicCalc

A physician’s own calculator.

What it is: a PREVENT cardiovascular risk calculator built by a practicing physician for clinical use, with physician, learner, and counseling modes.

Its boundary, in our own law’s words:

“a risk equation is not a treatment simulator”

— it supports clinical judgment and never replaces the evaluation of a doctor who knows the patient.

It runs in your browser and asks for no account.

Honesty note: the current live build predates our latest design system; a refreshed release is on the roadmap, and we say so here because stating what’s imperfect is part of being governed.

Findings & Fixes
